What we do

Three ways we check a payment system

  • 01 · audit

    Paid end-to-end audits

    We pay each endpoint a catalog lists and check its 402 offer, its network, its coin and its declared price against what it quotes. The listing is read too: whether its host resolves, where it is hosted, what its text claims.

  • 02 · fix

    Fixes in payment code

    Where a check finds a fault, we send the fix to the code that owns it.

  • 03 · hold

    Invariants and regression suites

    We write down what a payment flow must always hold and keep the tests that check it on every change.

Method

We pay, then we read

A payment endpoint is only checked once it has been paid. We send the payment it asks for, record what comes back, and set it beside what the endpoint and its listing claim.
